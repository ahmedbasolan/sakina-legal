Delete Your Account

Sakina app · Operated by Ahmed Basolan ("Service Provider")

You can permanently delete your Sakina account and all associated data at any time — from inside the app, or by requesting it here without needing the app installed.

Option 1 — Delete from within the app

  1. Open Sakina and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Delete Account.
  3. Confirm. Your account and data are removed immediately.

Option 2 — Request deletion without the app

Email ahmed.basolan97@gmail.com from the email address associated with your Sakina account, with the subject "Delete my Sakina account". We verify the request and delete your account within 5 business days.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes, without undue delay:

  • Your account (email, sign-in identity).
  • Your mood entries and spiritual history stored on our servers.
  • Your journey/path progress.
  • Your active subscription entitlement record (this does not automatically cancel a Google Play or App Store subscription — cancel that separately from the store you subscribed through, via Play Store → Payments & subscriptions or Apple's Subscriptions settings, to stop future billing).

Reflections and journal entries you've written are stored only on your device and are never on our servers — uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes those.
