Delete Your Account

Sakina app (com.lelahmed.sakina) · Operated by Ahmed Basolan ("Service Provider") · Last updated: 2026-08-27

You can delete your Sakina account and all its data at any time. The fastest way is inside the app, in two taps from Settings. If you have already uninstalled Sakina, you can request deletion by email instead — both routes are below.

Option 1 — Delete from inside the app

  1. Open Sakina and make sure you are signed in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm twice — Sakina asks you to confirm a second time, because this cannot be undone.

Your account is deleted from our servers immediately, the copy stored on your device is wiped at the same time, and you are signed out. Nothing is kept in a recoverable state and there is no grace period.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you no longer have the app installed, or you cannot sign in, email ahmed.basolan97@gmail.com from the email address associated with your Sakina account, with the subject line "Delete my account".

We will verify that the request comes from the account owner and complete the deletion within 30 days, usually much sooner. We will email you to confirm once it is done. We do not charge for this.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Crash and error diagnostics — if you had opted in to crash reporting, existing diagnostic reports are retained for up to 24 months from when they were collected, then deleted. These contain technical details only; they never contain your mood entries or journal content.
  • Purchase and subscription records — if you bought Sakina Pro, the record of that transaction is held by Google Play or the Apple App Store, not by us. We cannot delete it, and we are required to keep our own basic transaction record for tax and accounting purposes. To manage or cancel a subscription, use your Google Play or Apple App Store account. We never receive or store your payment card details.
  • Records we are legally required to retain — kept only where the law requires it, and only for as long as required.

Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription

Subscriptions are billed by Google Play or the Apple App Store, so they must be cancelled there. Cancel before deleting your account to avoid being charged again:

  • Android: Google Play app → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Sakina → Cancel
  • iOS: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Sakina → Cancel Subscription

A one-time lifetime purchase is not a subscription and does not need cancelling. It is also non-refundable once the account is deleted, and it cannot be restored afterwards.
